- In blender use `Store Named Attribute` in `Geometry Node` to store attribute with a name, and then you can pick that attribute in the `Shading mode` with `Attribute` node, or you can also connect the attribute to the `Output Group` in the `Geometry node`.
-
- In blender, Read attribute like `Normal` or `Position` in `Geometry node`works dynamically, it doesn't read the attribute from the `Group Input`.
-
- In blender, `Spline Parameter` node used to get the information about the line or a curve such as the position along the line.
-
- In blender, it is recommended to render the animation as a `PNG Sequence` rather than `Video`, to minimize error in case crash happening. And stitch them together in a `Video Sequencer` inside blender or using other software.
