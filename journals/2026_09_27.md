- Figma and Penpot are two of the best software to design the UI/UX of a web or an app. Figma is the industry standard, while Penpot is the open source alternative to Figma. Penpot for me is better not only it's free but because it reflects how web layout works.
- Even though they're using Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG) and capable of making a logo to some extent, neither of them are a replacement for an actual illustrator app like Adobe Illustrator or Inkscape. These apps are just meant to do illustration, logo, design printing and more. While Figma/Penpot are mainly built to solve collaboration problems while designing UI/UX for a web app.
- You can or should use both of them, for example use Inkscape to create complex vector illustrations, and then export them to Penpot.
