- If your Android Studio eats massive amount of resources and make your computer unresponsive, press `Alt+SysRq+F`. This will automatically kill the process with the highest amount of memory. You first need to enable this shortcut on linux first, before able to do said operation.
- After you force kill the process, usually the next time you open Android Studio, it will show errors, which has word like `lock` in there, you need to remove that lock file, if you installed the software using flatpak it located in `~/.var/app/com.google.AndroidStudio/config/Google/AndroidStudio2026.1.1/.lock` or you can execute this command to find all lock files in the flatpak installation of Android Studio `find ~/.var/app/com.google.AndroidStudio/ -name *.lock`, after you delete it, you can open the app normally.
